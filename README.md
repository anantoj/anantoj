## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **District Manager of Artificial Intelligence** at [PT Berca Hardayaperkasa](https://www.berca.co.id/), leading and overseeing AI-driven initiatives across multiple business domains. My work focuses on building and deploying production-grade AI solutions, including **Generative AI**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)**, **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**, and AI-powered enterprise automation.
